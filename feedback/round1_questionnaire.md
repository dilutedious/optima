# Optima — Round 1 questionnaire

Issued alongside prototype 0.1 on Sunday 19 April 2026 to evaluators E1, E2,
E3 and client (C).

## What's in this prototype

- Splash screen, login screen, signup screen, dashboard layout.
- **Mock data only** — three hard-coded tasks. You can't add your own yet.
- No real authentication. The login form accepts anything.
- No persistence: closing and re-opening resets everything.
- No weekly or monthly view.

Round 1 is about the *visual direction*. I want to know if the look and feel
matches what you'd expect from a study tool before I commit any more time to
the back end.

## Questions

1. First-impression read — what do you think this application is for, just
   from the splash and login screens?
2. Is the visual style appropriate for a study tool? Anything that feels
   wrong — colours, spacing, type?
3. On the dashboard, can you tell which task is the most important without
   reading every word?
4. What's the first thing you'd want to do that the prototype *doesn't* let
   you do?
5. Open notes — anything else worth saying.

## How to respond

Type into the shared Google Doc, or text me directly. Plain English is fine
— don't try to write a formal report. If something only takes a sentence,
write a sentence.

Deadline: by end of Tuesday 21 April so I can act on it for the week 2 sprint.
